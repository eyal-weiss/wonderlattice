# Historical portrait sources

The cameo faces use documented portraits or photographs of the named people. The little paper bodies and their motion are original CSS. The source images are bundled locally for offline play. Each card links to its image description on Wikimedia Commons; the images are displayed through a circular crop, with no facial features drawn over them.
